// how it works

Three steps.
One intelligent ping engine.

You describe it. AI enriches it. ProgressPing delivers it at the perfect moment.

01
Tell ProgressPing what matters to you
Add an event with a deadline, set a goal with a target, or drop in a task you've been putting off. No frameworks, no complex setup — just type it like you'd say it out loud.
30 seconds to add anything
02
AI enriches and plans automatically
In the background, ProgressPing's AI looks up what you'll need — fees, documents, deadlines, processing times. For goals, it builds a week-by-week roadmap. For tasks, it generates a step-by-step breakdown with time estimates for each.
Powered by Claude AI
03
Receive intelligent pings at the right time
ProgressPing calculates the ideal moment to notify you — not too early to ignore, not too late to act. Each ping arrives with context, a single clear next step, and a progress update. No noise. Just signal.
Email · Push · SMS coming soon
04
Log, adapt, and stay on course
Mark subtasks done, log your savings, update your progress. When you fall behind, AI recalculates your plan — no judgment, just a new path forward that still gets you to the finish line.
Plans that adjust to real life
