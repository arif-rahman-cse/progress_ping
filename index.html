Questions

Good questions,
short answers.

How is this different from a calendar or to-do list?
Calendars remind. To-do lists collect. ProgressPing thinks. It looks up what you'll need, breaks big tasks down, and sends pings timed to when you can act — not just when the day arrives.

What AI do you use?
Claude handles classification, enrichment, and planning. Your data never trains a third-party model. You can delete everything with one click.

How do pings arrive?
Email and web push today; iOS/Android push and SMS rolling out through Pro in Q3. You choose per-item which channels are active.

What if my plan changes?
Tell ProgressPing you're running behind, ahead, or want to pivot — it re-runs the plan and re-schedules pings. No shame, no streak loss, no rebuild.

Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Free forever with 5 items. Pro is month-to-month; Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Export your data any time as CSV or JSON.
